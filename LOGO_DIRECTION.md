\# MoreStars.co Logo Direction



\## Locked Logo Direction



Option 2 — Minimal Star



\---



\# Core Logo Psychology



The logo should communicate:

\- trust

\- reputation

\- growth

\- positive customer experiences

\- premium SaaS quality

\- simplicity

\- professionalism



The logo should NOT feel:

\- gimmicky

\- childish

\- flashy

\- cheap

\- overly corporate

\- cartoon-like



\---



\# Logo Style



Use:

\- clean geometric minimal star

\- modern SaaS typography

\- subtle premium appearance

\- scalable vector-style design

\- simple recognizable silhouette



Avoid:

\- giant gold stars

\- review badge styles

\- mascot branding

\- speech bubbles

\- clipart aesthetics



\---



\# Typography Style



Primary font direction:

Geist Sans



Characteristics:

\- modern

\- clean

\- readable

\- premium

\- trustworthy



\---



\# Preferred Color Direction



Primary Deep Navy:

\#0B1F33



Electric Blue:

\#2563EB



Teal Accent:

\#14B8A6



White:

\#FFFFFF



\---



\# Logo Layout



Preferred primary layout:



\[Minimal Star Icon] MoreStars



\---



\# Favicon + App Icon Direction



The minimal star icon should work independently as:

\- favicon

\- app icon

\- PWA icon

\- browser tab icon

\- QR branding element



The icon should remain recognizable at very small sizes.



\---



\# Dashboard Brand Feeling



The MoreStars identity should feel:

\- active

\- intelligent

\- premium

\- modern

\- easy

\- trustworthy



The interface should subtly communicate:

“This product is actively helping my business grow.”



\---



\# Strategic V1 Decision



Do not over-invest in logo perfection during V1.



Priority:

1\. Product UX

2\. Reliable flows

3\. Fast onboarding

4\. Dashboard quality

5\. Conversion

6\. Customer retention

7\. Brand polish refinement later



\---



\# Locked Direction



Minimal Star is officially locked as the MoreStars.co V1 brand direction.
