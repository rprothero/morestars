\# MoreStars.co Brand Foundation



\## Locked Brand Direction



MoreStars.co will use a Hybrid Light/Dark premium SaaS design.



The product should feel:

\- premium

\- polished

\- trustworthy

\- modern

\- alive

\- active

\- clean

\- simple

\- easy for small businesses to understand



\## Visual Style



\- Light main dashboard/workspace

\- Deep navy structure

\- Electric blue calls-to-action

\- Subtle teal glow accents

\- White premium cards

\- Soft gradients

\- Smooth animations

\- Clean spacing

\- Mobile-first responsiveness

\## Usage



Deep Navy:

Used for sidebar, headers, premium dark sections, footer, and structure.



Electric Blue:

Used for main buttons, links, active states, and primary actions.



Teal Accent:

Used lightly for success states, positive indicators, glow effects, and “alive” motion.



Soft Background:

Used for main dashboard background and page sections.



White:

Used for cards, forms, panels, and clean content areas.



Main Text:

Used for headings and body copy.



Muted Text:

Used for helper text, secondary labels, descriptions, and timestamps.



\## Customer-Facing Branding Rule



The customer review page should feel primarily branded to the business, not heavily branded to MoreStars.



Example:

ABC Plumbing

We’d love your feedback



MoreStars branding should be subtle, such as:

Powered by MoreStars



\## Product Feeling



When a business owner logs in, they should feel:



“This feels expensive.”

“This feels active.”

“This feels trustworthy.”

“This feels modern.”

“This feels easy.”

“This company knows what they’re doing.”



\## Locked Decision



Use Hybrid Light/Dark as the visual foundation for MoreStars.co V1.

\# Shadow Style



Use:

\- excessive movement

\- distracting effects
